Orders & Delivery

Your order is on its way. Most parcels arrive within about 15–20 days, though remote destinations can take longer. If an order hasn't arrived after 60 days (not counting 2–5 business days of processing), you're automatically eligible for a refund of the missing items. See our Shipping & Delivery page for details.

The rest is still in transit. When you order several items we may send them in separate packages, which helps keep prices low, shipping free, and lowers the chance of a parcel being held at customs. Each package arrives within the same delivery window.

"Completed" means we've finished processing your order and it has shipped, not that it has been delivered. It's now travelling to you. If you haven't received a shipping notification with tracking, please contact us.

Tracking

We email your tracking number as soon as your order ships. Some email providers send automated messages to spam, so check your junk folder too. You can also visit our Order Tracking page.

International parcels usually don't appear in your local postal service's system until the last few days of their journey. Until then, use an international tracking service rather than your local carrier's website.

With some carriers, tracking details take 2–5 business days to appear. If you still haven't heard from us after 5 days, contact us. Your order is very likely already on its way.

Cancellations, Returns & Sizing

We can only guarantee a cancellation if you contact us within 6 hours of ordering. We start preparing orders quickly, so after that window it's usually too late to stop them.

We ship internationally from our suppliers' warehouses, so we can't accept returns for a wrong size or a change of mind. Damaged or defective items are the exception and will be replaced. Read the full details in our Refunds & Returns policy.

Most items are cut to women's sizing, which tends to run smaller than men's. This matters most for shoes. Every product has its own size chart, so take your measurements and check it before ordering. If you're between two sizes, we suggest going up one or two.
