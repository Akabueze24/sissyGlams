Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information Sissy Glams collects when you visit our website or buy from us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1 Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • When you place an order or create an account, we collect your name, billing and shipping addresses, email address, and phone number.
  • We also receive details needed to take payment. Card and payment details are handled by our payment providers (see section 4).
  • If you contact us, we keep your messages so we can help you.

Information collected automatically

  • When you browse the site, we collect technical details about your device, such as your browser type, IP address, and time zone.
  • We also record how you use the site, including the pages and products you view and how you arrived here.
  • This is gathered using cookies, log files, and similar tools such as tags and pixels.

2 How We Use Your Information

  • To process, ship, and deliver your orders, and to keep you updated about them.
  • To manage your account and respond to your questions.
  • To keep the website secure and protect against spam, fraud, and abuse.
  • To understand how the site is used so we can improve our products and your shopping experience.
  • To send you news and offers, where you've agreed or where the law allows.

3 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

  • Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use them to keep your bag and account working and to understand how visitors use the site.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not work properly without them.
  • Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" signal. At present, we don't change how we collect or use data in response to it.
  • Learn more in our Cookies Policy.

4 Sharing Your Information

We only share your information with others when it's needed to run the store:

  • Payment providers process your payment securely. We don't store your full card details, and each provider handles your data under its own privacy policy.
  • Delivery partners receive your name, address, and phone number so they can deliver your order.
  • Analytics and security services help us understand site usage and block automated abuse.
  • Authorities, when we're legally required to respond to a lawful request or need to protect our rights.

5 Advertising & Marketing

  • We may use your information to show you ads or send you messages we think you'll enjoy.
  • You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in each email, or by contacting us.
  • You can also adjust or turn off personalised ads in the settings of the advertising platforms you use.

6 Your Rights

  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, and to ask us to correct, update, or delete it.
  • You may also be able to object to or limit certain uses of your information, or withdraw consent you've given.
  • To make a request, contact us using the details at the bottom of this page. If you're unhappy with how we handle it, you can complain to your local data protection authority.
  • Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including where our service providers operate.

7 Data Retention & Security

  • We keep your order information for as long as we need it to fulfil your orders, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. You can ask us to delete it where the law allows.
  • We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no online service can guarantee complete security.

8 Children's Privacy

  • Our website is not intended for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from minors.
  • If you believe a child has given us their information, please contact us and we'll delete it.

9 Third-Party Websites

  • Our site may link to other websites. A link isn't an endorsement, and we aren't responsible for the privacy practices of sites we don't operate.

10 Changes to This Policy

  • We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or for legal and operational reasons. The latest version is always on this page, with the date it was last updated.
