Refunds & Returns Policy

How cancellations, refunds, and replacements work at Sissy Glams.

6-Hour Cancellation

Cancel within 6 hours of placing your order.

Refunds in 5 Days

Approved refunds return to your original payment method within 5 business days.

Damaged? We Replace It

Defective or damaged items are reshipped once you send us photo proof.

Order Cancellations

We can only guarantee a cancellation if you contact us within 6 hours of placing your order. We start preparing orders quickly so they reach you sooner, which means that after this window your order may already be on its way and can no longer be cancelled.

Double-check your items, sizes, and shipping address before you pay. It's the easiest way to avoid needing a cancellation.

Payment processing fees

If your payment provider charged a processing fee on your original payment, that fee may be deducted from a cancellation refund.

Refunds

You can request a refund or reshipment if

  • Your order hasn't arrived within our guaranteed 60-day window (not counting 2–5 business days of processing).
  • You received the wrong item.
  • Tracking shows the parcel has been sent back to us.

We can't offer a refund if

  • Your order didn't arrive because of something within your control, such as an incorrect shipping address.
  • Delivery was held up by circumstances we can't control, like customs clearance or a natural disaster.
  • Other exceptional situations outside Sissy Glams' control.

When a refund is approved, you'll get a confirmation message and the credit is applied to your original payment method within 5 business days.

Exchanges & Returns

Because we ship internationally and directly from our suppliers' warehouses, we can't accept returns for a wrong size or a change of mind. Please check the size chart on each product before you order, and contact us if you're unsure.

Damaged or defective items

The one exception is an item that arrives damaged or defective. In that case we'll send a replacement, and you can keep the original.

  1. 1 Take a clear photo of the damaged or defective item.
  2. 2 Send it to us with your order number through our contact page.
  3. 3 We ship your replacement. If it's out of stock, you can choose a refund or a different item.
